Take quality photos without hiring a pro
@@1120
You don’t need to spend hundreds on a professional photographer to create a high-converting listing. With the right approach, you can take stunning photos yourself, even with a smartphone. The key is understanding what guests want to see and how to showcase your space in the best possible light.
@@1121
1. Clean and stage your space
@@1122
Before taking any pictures, make sure everything is spotless and styled. Open the curtains, fluff the pillows, and hide cords, visible bins and remotes. The photos must show the place as it really is, but you can remove items that make it look cluttered. Think of it like setting the stage; you want it to look welcoming, calm, and inviting.
@@1123
2. Lighting is everything
@@1124
Natural light is your best friend. Shoot during the day when your space is the brightest, ideally in the morning or late afternoon. Turn off overhead lights to avoid yellow tones. Open windows and let daylight in.
@@1125
3. Use a good phone or camera
@@1126
Most modern smartphones are more than enough. Just make sure your lens is clean and the resolution is set to high quality. Avoid using filters or heavy editing. Guests want to see the real space.
@@1127
4. Shoot horizontally
@@1128
Always take photos in landscape mode (horizontal), not vertical. Airbnb displays your listing photos in landscape, so this format gives a better view of each space.
@@1129
5. Capture wide angles
@@1130
Stand in corners or doorways to get as much of the room in the shot as possible. Use a wide-angle setting if available, but don’t distort the room. Guests want a real sense of space, not a fisheye view. On an iPhone Pro, the 0.5 lens works best.
@@1131
6. Include every important area
@@1132
Show everything: entryway, closets, appliances, amenities, parking, balcony, views. If it adds value, capture it. Lack of transparency leads to bad reviews.
@@1133
7. Your photo order matters (this is crucial for bookings).
@@1134
{1}Your first picture is your million-dollar shot.{2} It’s what sells your listing, and it matters more than anything else: it’s what brings in the clicks, the bookings and the income. Use your most eye-catching image as cover photo. Bedrooms usually perform best. Follow up with 4 key shots of different rooms that highlight your space’s best features. Airbnb shows only the first 5 photos before guests have to click “Show all photos,” so this is where you make a strong first impression and grab their attention.
