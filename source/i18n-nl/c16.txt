When to expand (and when to wait)
@@1869
Deciding to expand is one of the most exciting milestones in your hosting journey. {1}But growing too fast or too soon can backfire.{2} Be honest with yourself and tick what’s true today:
@@1870
Are you ready to expand?
@@1871
✓ You’re ready to expand if:
@@1872
{1}Your current listing runs like a well-oiled machine:{2} Systems are in place, guests are happy, reviews are great, and operations don’t depend on you 24/7.
@@1873
{1}You’ve built a reliable team:{2} Cleaners, a handyman, and a manager or co-host are already trained and capable of handling turnovers and guest issues without your direct involvement.
@@1874
{1}You’ve automated repetitive tasks:{2} Messaging, pricing, and check-ins run with minimal input thanks to tools like automated messaging, dynamic pricing tools, and smart locks.
@@1875
{1}You consistently turn a profit:{2} Your current property is generating reliable income, even during low season.
@@1876
{1}You have capital (or cash flow) to invest:{2} Expansion requires upfront costs. If you don’t have a buffer, the stress can outweigh the rewards.
@@1877
✕ You should wait if:
@@1878
{1}You’re still figuring out your systems:{2} If your current property requires constant attention or if things often go wrong, focus on refining your process first.
@@1879
{1}Your reviews are mixed or inconsistent:{2} Expanding while you're still trying to deliver a great guest experience can compound problems and hurt your reputation.
@@1880
{1}You don’t have a strong team in place:{2} Trying to manage two properties without support will lead to burnout and mistakes.
@@1881
{1}Cash flow is tight:{2} Stretching your budget can put pressure on the guest experience, maintenance, and quality, which hurts long-term success.
