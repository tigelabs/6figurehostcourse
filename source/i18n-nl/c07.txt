Explain that you’ll take out {1}rental insurance{2} and that you work with a {3}professional handyman{4} who fixes anything quickly. Tell them about your strict guest screening, house rules, and no-party policy. You use smart tools like noise monitors and security deposits to prevent issues.

@@880
You give them the same stable income, with more care for the property. The rent is guaranteed and on time, and if they ever want to go back to a traditional tenant, you handle the transition.

“Will this upset the neighbors?”
@@882
You screen every guest, enforce quiet hours and respectful behavior, and there’s always a local contact for emergencies and a direct line of communication.
