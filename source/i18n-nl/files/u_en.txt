AIRBNB CLEANING Checklists
@@244

Consistency is key to delivering a high-quality guest experience and that starts with a clear system. These checklists are designed to help you or your team follow the same process every time, ensuring your property stays spotless, functional, and welcoming.

You’ll find 3 types of checklists:

Turnover Cleaning: Daily or guest-checkout cleaning tasks to reset the space for the next arrival.

Deep Cleaning: Tasks that need to be done every 1–3 months (or as needed) to maintain long-term hygiene and property condition.

Pool & Garden Maintenance: Routine care to keep outdoor areas safe, clean, and beautiful—especially important in tropical climates.

Each checklist includes recommended frequencies, tools, and order of execution so cleaning is efficient and nothing gets missed. Print your checklists and display them in a visible spot in the property, preferably laminated so they’re reusable. Use dry-erase markers to tick off tasks without needing to reprint. You can also share them in your digital property wiki for easy access by your team.

Turnover checklist
@@246
 TURNOVER checklist
@@247
A turnover checklist is a clear, step-by-step guide your cleaner or team can follow every time a guest checks out. It ensures that all essential tasks are completed consistently from changing the sheets and refilling supplies, to checking for damage and getting the space ready for the next arrival. With a detailed checklist in place, you minimize mistakes, save time on back-and-forth communication, and maintain a high standard of cleanliness and presentation.

This checklist is also designed with an optimized flow. By following the steps in a specific order, your cleaner works more efficiently reducing the number of times they need to move in and out of each room. The goal is that it becomes a repeatable routine following the steps in the right order which saves time and energy with every turnover.

PRO TIP: add reference photos of the bed setup and key areas of the property. This ensures your team maintains consistency and the space always looks just like it does in the listing photos.

Linen & Guest Essentials: Everything your guests use directly—like towels, sheets, and pillow protectors.

Consistency with inventory keeps the quality high, prevents unnecessary stress, and ensures you’re always guest-ready.

Whether you have one property or ten, it shows how much each one earns and spends, which costs you can cut, and which property (or month) earns the most. Update it once a month and tax season gets a lot easier.
@@484
HOW TO USE IT
@@485
Name your properties
@@486
Open each property tab and type the property name at the top. The overview picks it up.
@@487
Set your currency
@@488
Type your currency (for example EUR, USD or BRL) in the Currency cell on the Overview tab.
@@489
Add your opening balance
@@490
In each property tab, enter the opening balance for the first month. This is usually your initial investment, so it is often a negative number.
@@491
Fill in the white cells every month
@@492
White cells are for your numbers: income per platform and each expense. Shaded cells calculate on their own, so don't type over them.
@@493
Read the Overview
@@494
The Overview tab adds up every property automatically: monthly profit, balance, quarters and the year. You don't fill in anything there.
