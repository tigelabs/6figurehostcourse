<p>Before signing a lease or investing in furniture, it’s crucial to understand your numbers. This Profit &amp; ROI Calculator helps you estimate how much income a property can generate and how long it will take to earn back your initial investment.</p>
<p>It’s designed to give you a quick, realistic snapshot of: monthly and yearly profit, return on investment (ROI), break-even timeline, and how your numbers shift at different occupancy rates.</p>
<div class="card">
  <div class="label">How to use it</div>
  <ol>
    <li><strong>Step 1:</strong> choose your currency and fill in your business expenses.</li>
    <li><strong>Step 2:</strong> pick your business model and enter your numbers under “Enter your data here”. The tables update automatically.</li>
    <li>Review the results at different occupancy rates (70%, 85%, 95%) to see how scenarios play out.</li>
  </ol>
  <p class="added">For more explanation of a field, tap “What is this?” under it. Your numbers are saved on this device.</p>
</div>
