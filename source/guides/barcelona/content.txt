::lesson ch1 | 01 | Barcelona by the numbers | Part 1 · Lesson 1 | Barcelona by the | numbers | Why demand never stops.
::part A city everyone wants to live in
<p>Barcelona is not only a holiday destination. It is a city people move to: to study, to work, to start a company, to try a new life by the sea. <strong>That constant flow of newcomers is the engine of the mid-term rental model.</strong></p>
<div class="vk-two">
<div class="vk-stat"><b>20M+</b><span>foreign tourists visited Catalonia in 2025, a record (INE).</span></div>
<div class="vk-stat"><b>10,101</b><span>licensed tourist apartments in Barcelona, all set to expire by November 2028.</span></div>
<div class="vk-stat"><b>€5</b><span>municipal tourist tax surcharge per night in 2026, rising to €8 by 2029.</span></div>
<div class="vk-stat"><b>32+</b><span>nights: the minimum stay that takes a rental out of the tourist category.</span></div>
</div>
<p>Look at what those numbers tell you. <strong>Tourist demand is enormous, but the supply of legal tourist flats is fixed and shrinking.</strong> Taxes on short stays are going up every year. Meanwhile, the people who come for a month, a semester or a year still need somewhere to live, and the long-term market is very hard to access for newcomers.</p>
::part Who rents for 32 days or more?
<p>In our experience, mid-term guests in Barcelona fall into a few clear groups:</p>
<div class="vk-grid3">
<div class="vk-card"><span class="vk-n">1</span><h4>Newcomers</h4><p>Looking for a long-term home. They just arrived, have no Spanish payslip or credit history yet, and need a base for one to three months while they search.</p></div>
<div class="vk-card"><span class="vk-n">2</span><h4>Students</h4><p>Erasmus, master’s programmes, business schools, language courses. Many stay a full semester.</p></div>
<div class="vk-card"><span class="vk-n">3</span><h4>Interns &amp; professionals</h4><p>Companies, startups and hospitals bring people for a few months.</p></div>
<div class="vk-card"><span class="vk-n">4</span><h4>Digital nomads</h4><p>Remote workers who want to live in the city for a season.</p></div>
<div class="vk-card"><span class="vk-n">5</span><h4>People in transition</h4><p>Renovating their home, waiting for a new flat, or in the city for medical treatment.</p></div>

<p>In Barcelona, the same apartment can be rented in three legally different ways. <strong>The type of rental depends on how long the guest stays and why.</strong></p>

<div class="gold"><span class="gold-k">Be careful</span><p><strong>The number of days alone is not enough.</strong> Since 2026, a seasonal rental must also have a real temporary reason, written in the contract and backed by documents. We explain exactly how in lesson 5.</p></div>

::lesson ch3 | 03 | Tourist licences &amp; 2028 | Part 1 · Lesson 3 | Tourist licences (HUT) and the | 2028 deadline | Is buying a flat with a licence still worth it?
::part What happens with tourist licences
<p>A tourist licence in Barcelona is called <strong>HUT</strong> (habitatge d’ús turístic). Without one, you cannot legally rent a flat to tourists for 31 nights or less. Here is the situation:</p>
<div class="vk-steps">
<div class="vk-step"><span class="vk-n">1</span><h4>No new licences</h4><p>Barcelona stopped granting new tourist-flat licences years ago. The number is frozen at around 10,101.</p></div>
<div class="vk-step"><span class="vk-n">2</span><h4>Licences belong to the property</h4><p>The only way to get one is to buy an apartment that already has it. These flats sell at a premium, often quoted at €60,000–120,000 above similar flats.</p></div>
<div class="vk-step"><span class="vk-n">3</span><h4>They end in November 2028</h4><p>In June 2024, Mayor Jaume Collboni announced that the city will not renew any of them, based on Catalan decree-law 3/2023, which lets municipalities in stressed areas limit or end tourist-flat permits.</p></div>
<div class="vk-step"><span class="vk-n">4</span><h4>The courts backed it</h4><p>In March 2025, Spain’s Constitutional Court upheld the Catalan framework.</p></div>
<div class="vk-step vk-wide"><span class="vk-n">5</span><h4>Fines are huge</h4><p>Fines for illegal tourist flats can reach <strong>€600,000.</strong></p></div>
</div>
::part Is a licence still worth it?
<p>Short-term rentals are very profitable in Barcelona, that has not changed. But you would be paying a premium for a licence that, unless the rules change, has around two years left. <strong>Run the numbers as if the licence ends in 2028:</strong> how much extra profit will you make until then, and does it pay back the premium you pay today?</p>

::group Part 2: The legal mid-term model
::lesson ch5 | 05 | Seasonal rentals done right | Part 2 · Lesson 5 | Seasonal rentals | done right | Stays of 32 days or more, under the 2026 Catalan law.
::part What the 2026 Catalan law requires
<p>For years, seasonal rentals were the “free zone” of the Spanish rental market. That changed in Catalonia with <strong>Law 11/2025</strong> of 29 December on housing and urban planning, in force since the start of January 2026. Here is what it means for you:</p>
<div class="vk-steps">
<div class="vk-step"><span class="vk-n">1</span><h4>A real temporary reason</h4><p>A seasonal rental is one made for professional, work, study or medical reasons, for provisional situations (waiting for a new home, returning to your usual residence) or similar temporary housing needs.</p></div>
<div class="vk-step"><span class="vk-n">2</span><h4>Write it in the contract</h4><p>The contract must state the temporary reason and the tenant’s permanent address.</p></div>
<div class="vk-step"><span class="vk-n">3</span><h4>Keep the proof</h4><p>Documents that prove the reason (work contract, enrolment letter, medical appointment…) are deposited with the tenant’s deposit (fianza) at the official Catalan deposit register (INCASÒL). <strong>If no temporary use is recorded there, the law presumes the rental is a permanent home.</strong></p></div>
<div class="vk-step"><span class="vk-n">4</span><h4>Rent caps apply</h4><p>In stressed residential market areas, which today cover almost all of Catalonia, seasonal rentals are subject to the same rent limits as permanent housing.</p></div>
<div class="vk-step vk-wide"><span class="vk-n">5</span><h4>Extensions are possible</h4><p>As long as the temporary reason continues and is documented in writing. A new contract with the same tenant for the same flat is treated as a permanent rental unless you can prove the temporary need still exists.</p></div>
</div>
<p class="warn">Holiday or leisure rentals are outside this law, but they must state it in the contract, and in Barcelona short stays for leisure need a tourist licence. <strong>Do not try to disguise a tourist stay as a seasonal one.</strong> It is exactly what inspectors look for.</p>
::part Your guests and their documents
<p>The good news: the typical mid-term guest in Barcelona already has a valid temporary reason. <strong>You just need to ask for it and keep it organised.</strong></p>
<div class="cmp-wrap"><table class="cmp"><thead><tr><th>Guest</th><th>Temporary reason</th><th>Document to keep</th></tr></thead><tbody>
<tr><th>Student / Erasmus</th><td>Study</td><td>Enrolment or acceptance letter with dates</td></tr>
<tr><th>Intern</th><td>Professional training</td><td>Internship agreement</td></tr>
<tr><th>Worker on assignment</th><td>Work</td><td>Work contract, letter from employer or assignment letter</td></tr>
<tr><th>Newcomer</th><td>Temporary need while finding a permanent home</td><td>New job contract in Barcelona and proof of home search, or similar</td></tr>
<tr><th>Patient / companion</th><td>Medical treatment</td><td>Appointment or treatment letter</td></tr>
<tr><th>Renovating their home</th><td>Provisional situation</td><td>Renovation permit or works contract</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>
<div class="gold"><span class="gold-k">Our process</span><p>We ask for the document <strong>before confirming the booking</strong>, we write the reason and the guest’s permanent address in the contract, and we deposit both with the fianza. <strong>It takes ten minutes per guest, and it is what keeps the whole business legal.</strong></p></div>
<div class="vk-tip"><b>Pro tip:</b> when you rent through Airbnb for 32+ nights, still sign your own seasonal contract with the guest. The platform booking alone does not include the clauses and documents the law asks for.</div>

::lesson ch6 | 06 | Renting by rooms &amp; the cap | Part 2 · Lesson 6 | Renting by rooms and the new | rent cap | The sum-of-rooms rule, and adding real services the right way.
::part The sum-of-rooms rule
<p>Renting by rooms is one of the most profitable ways to use a large apartment in Barcelona. A four-bedroom flat rented room by room has usually earned much more than the same flat rented as a whole. <strong>The regulators noticed.</strong></p>
<p>The same Law 11/2025 added a specific rule for room rentals (article 66 ter of the Catalan Housing Law). In a stressed residential area, when a dwelling is rented by rooms for housing purposes, <strong>the sum of all the room rents cannot exceed the maximum rent that would apply to the whole dwelling.</strong></p>
<div class="gold"><span class="gold-k">An example</span><p>Imagine the maximum legal rent for your whole flat is <strong>€1,800 per month.</strong> You have four rooms. Under the new rule, the four room rents together cannot go above €1,800, so an average of €450 per room. If you charged €900 per room (€3,600), you would be €1,800 over the cap.</p></div>
<p>Charging above the cap is a <strong>serious infringement</strong> when the excess is up to 30%, and a <strong>very serious infringement</strong> when it is more than 30%, under Catalan decree-law 1/2025.</p>

::part What this means in practice
<div class="vk-grid3">
<div class="vk-card"><span class="vk-n">1</span><h4>Know your cap first</h4><p>Before you buy or rent a flat. It depends on the official reference index for that flat (size, location, features). Your lawyer or the Catalan rent index tool can calculate it.</p></div>
<div class="vk-card"><span class="vk-n">2</span><h4>Capped, not banned</h4><p>Room rentals still work. <strong>The margins come from services, efficiency and the right flat,</strong> not from inflating rent.</p></div>
<div class="vk-card"><span class="vk-n">3</span><h4>Follow the news</h4><p>Some measures are being challenged in court and the details may change. Follow the news and your lawyer’s advice.</p></div>
</div>
::part Adding real services the right way
<p>Here is how we think about it. A mid-term room in our apartments is not just four walls. Our guests get a furnished room, internet, utilities, weekly cleaning of the shared areas, maintenance, a responsive manager and help settling into the city. <strong>Those are real services with real costs.</strong></p>
<p>Under the new rules, the rent is capped. Services that are genuinely provided, clearly described and priced transparently can be invoiced separately, for example:</p>
<div class="tiles">
<div><b>[[ic:broom]]Cleaning fee</b>Weekly cleaning of common areas, deep clean at check-out.</div>
<div><b>[[ic:phone]]Management fee</b>Guest support, coordination, maintenance requests, administration.</div>
<div class="vk-wide"><b>[[ic:sparkle]]Optional extras</b>Linen and towel service, extra room cleaning, airport pick-up.</div>
</div>
<div class="vk-two">
<div class="vk-card vk-no"><div class="vk-tag">The fine print (read this twice)</div><ul>
<li>The services must be real and actually delivered. A “fee” with nothing behind it is just disguised rent.</li>
<li>Catalan law warns that mandatory add-ons that, in practice, push the price above the cap can be treated as a breach. In stressed areas, the LAU also limits passing new expenses to the tenant that were not in the previous contract.</li>
<li>Services can have VAT consequences (lesson 8).</li></ul></div>
<div class="vk-card vk-yes"><div class="vk-tag">Our approach</div><ul>
<li>We keep the rent within the cap.</li>
<li>We price services at what they really cost us plus a fair margin.</li>
<li>Each service and its price is written in the contract, separate from the rent. Where possible, extras are optional.</li>
<li>Have your lawyer review your services menu before you use it.</li></ul></div>
</div>
<blockquote class="pullquote">“Charge for value you deliver, never for a loophole.”</blockquote>
<p>Before you sign a flat, run its full monthly numbers in <a href="#ch10">lesson 10</a>. If the numbers only work above the cap, it is not a deal.</p>

::lesson ch7 | 07 | Platforms &amp; enforcement | Part 2 · Lesson 7 | Platforms, registration and | enforcement | What changed in 2025 and 2026.
::part Platforms and registration in 2026
<p>Most of our mid-term guests find us on platforms: Airbnb (with monthly stays), and specialist sites for mid-term rentals and students. Here is what changed recently:</p>
<div class="vk-steps">
<div class="vk-step"><span class="vk-n">1</span><h4>The national registry was annulled</h4><p>From July 2025, Spain required a single registration number (NRUA) for tourist and seasonal listings. In May 2026, the Supreme Court annulled it, ruling that the State overstepped regional powers. The digital data-sharing system for platforms remains.</p></div>
<div class="vk-step"><span class="vk-n">2</span><h4>Regional rules still apply</h4><p>In Catalonia, tourist flats need their HUT registration, and seasonal rentals must follow Law 11/2025 (contract, documents, deposit).</p></div>
<div class="vk-step"><span class="vk-n">3</span><h4>EU rules arrived</h4><p>The EU Short-Term Rental Regulation (2024/1028) applies from 20 May 2026. Platforms must share activity data with authorities, display registration numbers where required, and remove listings when authorities order it.</p></div>
<div class="vk-step"><span class="vk-n">4</span><h4>Enforcement is real</h4><p>Spanish authorities fined Airbnb €64 million and Barcelona has a long record of closing illegal flats.</p></div>
</div>
<div class="gold"><span class="gold-k">What this means for you</span><p><strong>Your listings and your contracts must tell the same story.</strong> If your listing says “monthly stays for professionals and students”, your contracts, documents and deposits must prove it.</p></div>
<div class="vk-tip"><b>Pro tip:</b> set your minimum stay on every platform to 32 nights. One accidental 10-night booking is enough to turn a legal seasonal rental into an illegal tourist one.</div>
::task Set the minimum stay to 32 nights on every platform you use

::lesson ch8 | 08 | Individual or company? | Part 2 · Lesson 8 | Individual or company? | (taxes explained) | IRPF, corporate tax, VAT and dividends.
::part Starting as an individual
<p>If you start with one apartment or a few rooms, renting as an individual (persona física) is usually simplest. You declare the income in your annual personal income tax return (IRPF).</p>
<div class="vk-grid3">
<div class="vk-card"><h4>Progressive tax</h4><p>Net rental income (rent minus deductible costs) is added to your other income. For 2026, the state brackets go from 9.5% to 24.5%, plus the regional part. <strong>In Catalonia the top combined marginal rate reaches 50%.</strong></p></div>
<div class="vk-card"><h4>Deductible costs</h4><p>Mortgage interest, community fees, IBI (property tax), insurance, repairs, utilities you pay, management and cleaning services, and depreciation of the building and furniture.</p></div>
<div class="vk-card"><h4>No rental reduction</h4><p>The 50% reduction is for renting a habitual home. <strong>Seasonal rentals do not qualify.</strong> Rooms rented as the tenant’s habitual residence can qualify, which is a different model.</p></div>
</div>
<p class="warn">If you are not a Spanish tax resident, different rules (non-resident income tax) apply. Talk to an advisor.</p>
<p>This works well while the numbers are small. But because IRPF is progressive, <strong>every extra apartment you add is taxed at a higher marginal rate.</strong> With two or three properties, the tax bill can grow faster than the business.</p>
::part When a company makes sense
<p>Once you have two or three properties, or you plan to keep growing, it often makes sense to operate through a limited company (Sociedad Limitada, SL). <strong>That is what we recommend to anyone who wants to build this as a real business,</strong> fully legal and organised.</p>
<div class="cmp-wrap"><table class="cmp"><thead><tr><th>Company type</th><th>Corporate tax rate 2026</th></tr></thead><tbody>
<tr><th>General rate</th><td>25%</td></tr>
<tr><th>Micro-companies</th><td>19% on the first €50,000 of profit, 21% on the rest (turnover under €1M)</td></tr>
<tr><th>Newly created</th><td>15% (first years with profit)</td></tr>
<tr><th>Asset-holding (patrimonial)</th><td>25%, reduced rates do not apply</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>
<p class="warn"><strong>Important:</strong> a company that only owns and rents property, without a real economic activity, can be considered an asset-holding company and lose the reduced rates. For rental activity, tax rules generally look for at least one employee working full time in the business. Ask your advisor how to structure it.</p>
<div class="chips"><span>✓ Flat corporate rate instead of up to 50%</span><span>✓ Reinvested profit stays in the company</span><span>✓ Professional structure: contracts, insurance, financing, partners</span><span>✓ Personal tax only when you take money out</span></div>
::part VAT: when it applies
<p>VAT (IVA) is the part most hosts get wrong, so let’s make it simple.</p>
<div class="vk-grid3">
<div class="vk-card"><span class="vk-n">1</span><h4>Plain rental is exempt</h4><p>If you only rent the space (even furnished, with cleaning at check-in and check-out), you do not charge VAT. The downside: you also cannot recover the VAT you pay on furniture, renovations or services.</p></div>
<div class="vk-card"><span class="vk-n">2</span><h4>Hotel-type services add VAT</h4><p>Services typical of the hotel industry during the stay, such as periodic cleaning of the guest’s space or regular changes of bedding and towels, make it accommodation subject to VAT (reduced 10% rate). Optional extras sold separately are taxed as independent services.</p></div>
<div class="vk-card"><span class="vk-n">3</span><h4>Charge VAT, deduct VAT</h4><p>A company that charges VAT on its services can deduct the VAT it pays on furniture, renovations, cleaning, supplies and professional fees, and in some cases get it refunded. <strong>On a full renovation and furnishing, that can be thousands of euros.</strong></p></div>
</div>
<div class="gold"><span class="gold-k">Decide before you renovate</span><p>Which option is better depends on your guests (individuals cannot deduct VAT, companies can), your investment plan and your service model. <strong>The VAT you can recover depends on how you set up the activity from the start.</strong> VAT is declared quarterly (form 303) and annually (form 390). Keep every invoice in the company’s name.</p></div>
::part Dividends and a worked example
<p>When you take profits out of your company as dividends, they are taxed in your personal savings income. In 2026 the rates are 19% up to €6,000, 21% up to €50,000, 23% up to €200,000, 27% up to €300,000 and 30% above. The company withholds 19% when it pays the dividend.</p>

::task Run the numbers for every flat on your shortlist
::done You can judge any Barcelona flat on its real numbers.
::endlesson

::lesson ch11 | 11 | Take it to other cities | Part 3 · Lesson 11 | Take the model to other | European cities | Barcelona is your training ground.
::part Barcelona is your training ground
<p>Here is the best part: <strong>once you understand this model in Barcelona, you understand it in most of Europe.</strong> Big European cities are all fighting the same battle: housing shortages, overtourism and pressure from residents. The tools change name, but they look very similar:</p>
<div class="vk-two">
<div class="vk-card"><h4>[[ic:calendar]]Night caps</h4><p>Amsterdam, for example, allows only 30 nights per year for primary residences.</p></div>
<div class="vk-card"><h4>[[ic:key]]Licences and registration</h4><p>And principal-residence rules in cities like Paris, Berlin and Lisbon.</p></div>
<div class="vk-card"><h4>[[ic:refresh]]Platform data sharing</h4><p>Since 20 May 2026, the EU Short-Term Rental Regulation makes platforms share data with authorities and remove listings when ordered.</p></div>
<div class="vk-card"><h4>[[ic:alert]]Rent controls</h4><p>Spreading from long-term to seasonal rentals, as in Catalonia.</p></div>
</div>
<p>And almost everywhere, the same opportunity: <strong>people who move to a city for work, study or a new life need a furnished place for a few months.</strong> That demand does not depend on tourism, and in most countries these rentals are treated as housing, not as tourist accommodation.</p>
<p>Rules are generally softer in smaller cities and regions with lower population pressure. Coastal towns, university cities and secondary cities often have fewer restrictions, lower purchase prices and less competition, while still having universities, hospitals and companies that bring mid-term guests.</p>
<blockquote class="pullquote">“Learn the model in the hardest city. Then take it wherever the rules are kinder.”</blockquote>

::lesson ch12 | 12 | Mistakes to avoid | Part 3 · Lesson 12 | Mistakes to avoid in | Barcelona | We learned some of these the hard way. Learn them the easy way.

<div class="mk-grid">
<div class="mk"><span class="mk-x">✕</span><h4>[[ic:calendar]]Stays under 32 nights</h4><p>Accepting them without a tourist licence. Set your minimum stay everywhere.</p><a href="#ch7">Platforms, Lesson 7 →</a></div>
<div class="mk"><span class="mk-x">✕</span><h4>[[ic:alert]]“Seasonal” as a label only</h4><p>Without a real reason and documents, the law now presumes it is a permanent home.</p><a href="#ch5">Seasonal rentals, Lesson 5 →</a></div>
<div class="mk"><span class="mk-x">✕</span><h4>[[ic:key]]Ignoring the rent cap on rooms</h4><p>Calculate the cap of the whole flat first.</p><a href="#ch6">Rent cap, Lesson 6 →</a></div>
<div class="mk"><span class="mk-x">✕</span><h4>[[ic:sparkle]]Inventing fees</h4><p>To get around the cap. Only charge for real services, transparently.</p></div>
<div class="mk"><span class="mk-x">✕</span><h4>[[ic:box]]Paying for a licence with no plan</h4><p>A big premium for a tourist licence without a plan for after November 2028.</p><a href="#ch3">Licences, Lesson 3 →</a></div>
