2044|What do the columns mean?
2045|<strong>Occupancy rate.</strong> The occupation rate is the rental percentage, like how many days your accommodation is rented vs empty. For example: if your property gets booked for 15 days in the month and the other 15 days are not rented, your occupation rate is 50%. Worst-case, average and best-case are the worst, average and best occupancy in the zone where you are going to start. You can change each percentage.
2046|<strong>Monthly income.</strong> How much you would earn per month before deducting any expenses — also called Monthly Gross Profit.
2047|<strong>Monthly costs.</strong> The costs to manage an Airbnb property: cleaners, cleaning products, handyman services, utility bills (water, electricity, gas, internet) ... You can change them in step 1.
2048|<strong>Monthly profit.</strong> Your estimated income after subtracting all recurring monthly costs, such as rent, team, utilities and cleaning costs.
2049|<strong>ROI.</strong> This is the annual return of investment in percentage.
2050|<strong>Break even.</strong> How long (in months) it will take to recover your initial investment.

2078|*A leasehold is when you rent a property long-term, often for several years, with the right to use it as your own.
