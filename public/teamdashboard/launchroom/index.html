From launches that worked

Rules we follow

  • Story first, offer second. Jeff Walker's Product Launch Formula runs pre-prelaunch, prelaunch content (usually three pieces: teach, story, proof), open cart, then post-launch. Our story, case study and "start from zero" are those three pieces.
  • Open cart is its own sequence. Walker suggests 5–7 days of open cart. Ours is 4 days, so every day needs one clear email and one clear post.
  • One call to action per email. Each email sends people to the course page. Nothing else.
  • The last day is the biggest day. Launch affiliate Matt McWilliams sees 40–60% of sales on the final day, and Kajabi and Copyhackers case studies show the same spike. Don't panic on Saturday.
  • Email 2–3 times on the last day. Recommended by Jack Born on Amy Porterfield's podcast, because people get busy and miss one email.
  • The deadline is real. The price goes back to €297 at midnight, and it really does. Never extend it.
  • Expect the mid-cart dip. Sales usually slow on days 2–3. That's when we post the objections content and answer questions by email and in the Facebook group.
  • Remove buyers from sales emails. Exclude purchasers in Brevo before every send, so buyers only get the welcome.
  • Close the loop. After the cart closes, welcome buyers fast and thank everyone else. Then write down what worked while it's fresh.

Will it always be easy? No. Is it possible? 100%.

Sources: Jeff Walker's open cart (systeme.io) · Matt McWilliams, closing the final day · Amy Porterfield with Jack Born · Kajabi, 2x your next launch · Copyhackers on last-day sales
