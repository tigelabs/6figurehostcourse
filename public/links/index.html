A tool from lesson 2 of our course. Type your city, copy the prompt and paste it into your AI assistant. In a minute you know what is allowed: short, mid and long-term rentals, and subletting.

Can you help me find the current rules for renting out a property in [City, Country]? I want to understand the rules for short-term rentals (under 1 month), mid-term rentals (1 to 6 months) and long-term rentals, and for subletting a property I rent (rental arbitrage). For each type of rental, I want to know: - If it is legal, and under which conditions - Whether I need a license, permit or registration - Any minimum or maximum stay rules, or limits on the number of nights per year - Tax obligations (income tax, tourist or occupancy tax) - Zoning restrictions, primary residence rules or building/HOA rules - What a landlord needs to agree to if I want to sublet - Whether I can do this as a private individual, or need to register as self-employed or a company Please include any helpful links or resources to official government sites if available. At the end, add a short summary I can copy into my notes, in this format: - Short-term rentals (under 1 month): allowed / not allowed / with conditions - Mid-term rentals (1–6 months): allowed / not allowed / with conditions - Long-term rentals: allowed / not allowed / with conditions - Subletting (rental arbitrage): allowed / not allowed / with conditions - License or permit needed: yes / no (and which one) - Minimum stay: ... - Taxes: ... - Other important rules: ...

AI can make mistakes. Always confirm the answer on the official government sites it links to.
