1Purpose and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how 6 Figure Host, run by Farah & Daniel ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information when you buy or use the 6-Figure Host Course, its bonus materials and templates (the "Course"), and when you contact us or follow us on social media.

It applies when you:

  • buy the Course through our checkout;
  • log in to and use the Course;
  • send us a review, feedback or a message;
  • open emails we send you about your purchase or the Course;
  • use templates we share through Google Drive or Canva, or join our community.

It does not cover websites and platforms run by others, even when the Course links to them (for example Airbnb, Booking.com, Canva, Google, Instagram or Facebook). Their own privacy policies apply there.

2Age requirements

The Course is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If we learn that we have, we delete it promptly. If you believe this has happened, email us at contact@6figurehost.com.

3Legal bases for processing

We only process your personal information when we have a valid reason to do so:

  • Contract. To complete your purchase, send you your access code and give you access to the Course.
  • Legitimate interests. To keep the Course secure, prevent misuse of access codes, answer your questions and improve the Course, as long as this does not override your rights.
  • Consent. For marketing emails and for sharing your review on our social media. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation. To keep records required by tax and accounting laws.

4Information we collect

  • Contact details: your name and email address, and the billing details you enter at checkout.
  • Purchase details: what you bought, the order number, date and amount, and whether the order was refunded.
  • Access information: your personal access code, the date of your last login and an anonymous identifier for each device you log in with (up to 5 per code). We use these only to give you access and to prevent shared codes.
  • Course activity: your progress, ticked tasks, choices and the notes you type in the Course are saved on your own device (see section 6). We receive only a short progress summary: which lessons you marked as done, how many tasks you ticked, the last page you opened, your language and when you were last active. We also keep a short log of your logins (date, device number and approximate country and city from your connection). Your notes and answers never leave your device.
  • Messages, reviews and feedback: what you send us through the Course, by email or in Instagram direct messages, including your name if you add it.
  • Technical data: basic information your browser sends when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request, which our hosting provider uses to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.
We never see or store your full payment card details. Payments are handled by our checkout and payment providers.

5Where your information comes from

  • From you, when you buy the Course, log in, send feedback or contact us.
  • From our payment provider (Stripe), which tells us that your order was paid or refunded.
  • From social platforms, when you message us on Instagram or join our Facebook community.
  • Automatically, through the strictly necessary cookie and the technical data described in sections 4 and 6.

6Cookies and local storage

We keep this to the minimum the Course needs to work:

  • Login cookie (strictly necessary). After you enter your access code, a secure cookie keeps you logged in on that device for about a year. Without it, the Course cannot work, so it cannot be switched off.
  • Local storage on your device. Your progress, ticked tasks, chosen currency and notes are saved in your own browser, so you can pick up where you left off. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • Embedded and linked content. Some pages show videos or link to services such as YouTube, Google Drive, Google Forms or Canva. Those services may set their own cookies when you use them.

We do not use advertising, retargeting or analytics cookies. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

Affiliate links. Some links in the Course are affiliate links (for example to create an Airbnb listing). If you use them, the partner may track the referral and we may receive a commission, at no extra cost to you.

7How we use your information

  • to process your purchase and send your receipt and access code;
  • to give you access to the Course and keep access codes from being shared or misused;
  • to answer your questions and support requests;
  • to send you important updates about the Course;
  • to send you news and offers, if you agreed to receive them (you can unsubscribe in any email);
  • to read your feedback and improve the Course;
  • to share your review on our social media, only if you gave us permission;
  • to meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations and to protect our rights.

8Who we share your information with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We only share it with service providers that help us run the Course:

  • Stripe to take payments and process refunds.
  • Brevo (email), to send your access code, Course updates and, if you agreed, our newsletter.
  • Cloudflare (hosting), to host the Course, store access codes and protect the site.
  • Google (Drive, Forms and Sheets), to share templates and to receive feedback you send us through the Course.
  • Canva, when you open a Canva template we share.
  • Meta (Instagram and Facebook), when you message us or join our community there.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it (for example a court order), to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a sale or restructuring of our business, in which case we will tell you about any important change.

9International data transfers

Our service providers may process your information in the United States, the European Union or other countries. Where the law requires it, these transfers are protected by safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

10Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your information, unless we must keep it by law;
  • receive a copy of your information in a common format (portability);
  • object to or restrict certain processing, including direct marketing;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing before that;
  • opt out of any sale or sharing of personal information (we do neither);
  • not be discriminated against for using these rights.

If you live in the EU or UK, you can also file a complaint with your local data protection authority. If you live in California, you also have the right to know which categories of information we collect, where they come from and who we share them with, as described in this policy.

To use any of these rights, email contact@6figurehost.com. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we will reply within the time the law requires (generally 30 days in the EU and UK, 45 days in California). If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer with the subject line "Privacy rights appeal".

11Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

We do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of. We still treat a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal from your browser as a valid opt-out request where the law requires it. There is no common standard for Do Not Track signals yet; we will update this policy if one is adopted.

12Data retention

  • Purchase and access records: for as long as you have access to the Course, plus up to 7 years for tax and accounting purposes.
  • Marketing emails: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your data.
  • Feedback and messages: for as long as they are useful to answer you and improve the Course.
  • Progress and notes in the Course: on your own device, until you clear your browser data. The progress summary and login log we receive: for as long as you have access to the Course.

After that, we delete or anonymize the information, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

13Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, such as encrypted connections, signed login cookies and limited access to our tools. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your access code private: it is personal to you.

14Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version. If we make important changes, we will tell you by email or in the Course.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your personal information? Write to us at contact@6figurehost.com.
