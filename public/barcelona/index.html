Barcelona isone of the best markets in Europe

It is also one of the strictest. New tourist licences are not issued, the ones that exist end in November 2028, and since January 2026 mid-term rentals are regulated too. Operating an illegal tourist flat can cost up to €600,000.

Does that mean you should not invest? No. It means you need to understand the rules better than everyone else. Every time the rules get stricter, the people who do it right get less competition.

We run five apartments in Barcelona without a single tourist licence. This guide is how.
