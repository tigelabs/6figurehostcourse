But Bali also eats unprepared investors for breakfast. You cannot own land as a foreigner. The zone of a plot matters more than the view. Many villas are built to be sold, not to last. And unlicensed rentals are being delisted from the platforms.

Does that mean you should not invest? No. It means the bar is higher, and that is exactly your opportunity. When the rules get tighter, the hosts who did it right get less competition, better rankings and higher prices.
