Our storyFrom sleeping on the couch to homes around the world

Where it startedFrom sleeping on the couch behind a beach blanket and subletting my own bedroom…

  1. A suitcase and a little savings

    In our early twenties we left Belgium and Brazil for Barcelona. No safety net, no family nearby, just the belief that there had to be more to life than doing things the “normal” way.

  2. Housemates in a converted closet

    In 2017 Farah rented a room from Daniel. He was sleeping in a walk-in closet turned bedroom, so he could rent out the bigger rooms. That is how we met.

  3. €67.50 left in the bank

    When Farah got her own place, the deposit left her almost broke. She built a bed from pallets, sublet her bedroom and slept on the couch behind a beach blanket. It wasn't glamorous, but it worked.

  4. Wait a second… let's go all in

    We did everything ourselves, down to YouTube tutorials on fixing a toilet. Then we built systems: self check-in, automated messages, our first cleaner. One apartment turned into two, then five, all legal with stays of 31 nights or more.

  5. Then came Bali

    We put all our savings into a very basic villa. Scary AF. We turned it into a three-bedroom boutique stay, and today we run three properties there. The wildest part? We still don't own a single one of them.

Will it always be easy? No. Is it possible? 100%.
